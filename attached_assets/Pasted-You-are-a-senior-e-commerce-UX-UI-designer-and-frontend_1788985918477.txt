- Preserve RTL behavior.

- Use a bottom sheet/modal when appropriate.

SORT:
- Make sorting a compact control.

==================================================
13. UX PRINCIPLES
==================================================

Follow these principles strictly:

- Product discovery before filtering complexity.
- One primary action per product.
- Visual hierarchy must be obvious within 1 second.
- Reduce cognitive load.
- Reduce unnecessary scrolling.
- Make prices immediately scannable.
- Make categories immediately understandable.
- Never cover content with floating UI.
- Never duplicate labels.
- Never make secondary actions compete with primary actions.
- Do not design for visual novelty; design for effortless shopping.

==================================================
14. FINAL QUALITY CHECK
==================================================

Before considering the redesign complete, test the page as a real mobile shopper.

Ask:

1. Can I reach the first product quickly?
2. Can I understand the categories instantly?
3. Can I scan 4–6 products quickly?
4. Can I identify the price immediately?
5. Is "Add to Cart" obviously the primary action?
6. Can I open product details without a large secondary button?
7. Are filters compact and understandable?
8. Does anything overlap the content?
9. Are category names correct and non-duplicated?
10. Does the entire page feel like ONE professional e-commerce design system?

If any answer is NO, fix the UI before finishing.

Do not add decorative elements just to make the page look richer.

The final result should feel:
MINIMAL
FAST
PREMIUM
TRUSTWORTHY
MOBILE-FIRST
EASY TO SCAN
CONVERSION-FOCUSED
