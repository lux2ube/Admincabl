## Relationship notes

- **products** is the hub: linked to categories (via product_categories), tags (via product_tags), attributes (via product_attributes → variant_attribute_values → attribute_values), variants, galleries, sells, order_items, card_items, product_shippings, product_coupons.
- **customers** links to customer_addresses, orders, cards.
- **orders** links to order_items, order_statuses, coupons.
- **staff_accounts** links to roles via staff_roles (many-to-many).
- **categories** is self-referencing via parent_id (subcategories).
- Junction/pivot tables (no own surrogate PK shown): product_tags, product_categories, product_attributes, product_coupons, product_shippings, staff_roles.
