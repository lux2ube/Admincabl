---
name: CABL editorial content
description: Rules for CairoVolt-inspired editorial pages in the CABL Yemen storefront.
---

Editorial pages may preserve the shared CABL/CairoVolt-inspired structure, but their commerce claims must come from the project catalog, shipping options, payment methods, and order data. Do not invent contact details, warranty durations, delivery promises, test results, or Egypt-specific facts.

**Why:** The storefront is authorized to reuse the visual language and page patterns, not CairoVolt's Egypt-specific business claims. Unsupported claims would make the clone misleading.

**How to apply:** Keep editorial copy reusable and generic when the database has no corresponding setting. Link guides and solution pages to live products, and state clearly when a feature such as automated warranty verification still needs a backend source.
